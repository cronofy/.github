## Embedded Scheduling Infrastructure For Your Applications

Building scheduling infrastructure and workflows from scratch isn’t easy, which is why Cronofy is chosen by high-performance engineering teams who need scheduling features designed to operate at scale. Our APIs and tools enable developers and product managers to integrate real-time calendar synchronization, availability management, and scheduling workflows seamlessly into their products.

## Cronofy Products

[**Cronofy White-Label API**](https://www.cronofy.com/developer?utm_source=github&utm_medium=profile&utm_content=products-white-label-api)

Access a unified API to interact with multiple calendar services, manage events, and handle complex availability queries - great for teams who want full control over their scheduling workflow.

[**Cronofy Embedded Scheduler**](https://www.cronofy.com/developer/scheduler-api?utm_source=github&utm_medium=profile&utm_content=products-embedded-scheduler)

Embed fully managed scheduling into your website or application that supports multi-person, multi-event, rescheduling, and complex selection rules - great for teams wanting to launch quickly with pre-built elements.

[**Cronofy Scheduler**](https://www.cronofy.com/scheduler-for-recruitment-teams?utm_source=github&utm_medium=profile&utm_content=products-scheduler)

A standalone scheduling solution built for recruitment teams that simplifies appointment booking and calendar management. 

## Getting Started

You can get started with Cronofy instantly - no credit card, no sales calls. 

To begin integrating Cronofy into your application:

1. **Create a Developer Account:** Create your account [here](https://app.cronofy.com/sign_up/developer?utm_source=github&utm_medium=profile&utm_content=started-create-dev-account) to obtain your API credentials.
2. **Review the Documentation:** Explore our [Getting Started Guide](https://docs.cronofy.com/developers/getting-started/?utm_source=github&utm_medium=profile&utm_content=started-review-documentation) for step-by-step instructions on setting up and using our APIs. 
3. **Test with Postman:** Download our [Postman Collection](https://docs.cronofy.com/developers/getting-started/postman/?utm_source=github&utm_medium=profile&utm_content=started-postman) to explore our APIs and make your first API calls.
